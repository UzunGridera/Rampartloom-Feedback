# Feedback guidelines

Thank you for helping improve Rampartloom.

- Search existing tickets before opening a new one.
- Keep one problem or suggestion per ticket.
- Use a clear English title so reports can be grouped and searched.
- Describe what happened and what you expected without attaching logs or raw
  diagnostic files.
- Remove personal or account information from screenshots before attaching
  them.
- Be respectful to testers and developers.

This is a beta feedback tracker, not an emergency or guaranteed-response
support channel. Public submissions may be moderated or removed when they
contain unsafe, private, abusive or unrelated content.
