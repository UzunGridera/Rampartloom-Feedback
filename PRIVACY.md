### Data, purposes and collection

The game collects data electronically through your actions, the game client, servers and connected services:

- **Local profile and settings:** your chosen display name, portrait, audio/video/accessibility preferences, tutorial progress and local practice statistics are saved on your computer to provide those features. Local bot practice can use aggregate gameplay choices and recent build positions. Choose a nickname rather than a real name.
- **Online identity and sessions:** Steam identifiers and authentication tickets, Unity player identifiers and session tokens authenticate you and connect you to parties, matchmaking and matches. The game does not ask for your Steam password. Network services process connection information, including IP addresses and technical session identifiers, to route traffic and maintain the service.
- **Multiplayer information:** display name, portrait, faction, team/role, gameplay actions and match state are shared with the server and relevant participants. Party text chat is transmitted to other party members through the session service. Do not put private information in chat or your display name. The current game has no built-in voice chat or microphone recording.
- **Match and ranking records:** our server can retain player identifiers, display names, ratings, results, match times, contribution statistics and rating history to operate rankings, preserve match results and investigate faults or abuse. Leaderboard names and ratings can be visible to other players.
- **Diagnostics:** the game writes local match/performance and session-health reports. Engine or operating-system logs can contain device/system details, errors and network information. Unity engine and online services may also process diagnostic information under their own notices. We do not promise that using the game produces no telemetry.
- **Support and feedback:** if you email us, we receive your address and the information you send. “SEND BETA FEEDBACK” opens a GitHub page after confirmation; it does not automatically upload your files or diagnostics. GitHub issues are public. Keep account identifiers, emails, logs and private details out of public reports; use email for privacy requests.

We use necessary account/session information to provide the game features you request; reliability and security records to protect and maintain the service; and support information to answer requests. Depending on applicable law, the bases are performance of the service contract, legitimate interests in reliable and secure operation that do not override your rights, compliance with legal obligations, and establishment or protection of legal claims. Where a separate consent is legally necessary, this notice does not replace it. Under Turkish Law No. 6698, the relevant grounds are Article 5(2)(c), (ç), (e) and (f), as applicable to those purposes.

Providers and their infrastructure may process information outside your country, including outside Türkiye. Contact us for information about transfers involving your data and applicable safeguards.

Provider information: [Steam privacy policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/), [Unity player privacy policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy), [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Contabo privacy policy](https://contabo.com/en/legal/privacy/).

We do not sell player data or use it for targeted advertising. Prerecorded AI-assisted art, music, voices and sound effects are shipped as assets; playing does not send your gameplay or chat to a generative-AI service.

### Storage, deletion and your choices

Local settings remain until reset or removed. The game's local report index is limited to the most recent 100 reports; engine/operating-system logs have separate lifecycles. **Settings → DELETE LOCAL DATA** clears game-owned preferences and local report folders after confirmation. It does **not** delete your Steam account, Unity account, server-side ratings/match history, provider records or all operating-system logs.

Server match/rating records currently have no automatic age-based expiry. They remain until removed following a request or an operational retention review; records needed for unresolved security incidents, legal obligations or claims may need to be retained. Support correspondence is retained while needed to resolve the request and related obligations. You can request a review and deletion of identifiable records by email. Provider-controlled records are also subject to the provider's own retention and request processes.

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing, withdraw a consent without affecting prior lawful processing, and complain to the competent data-protection authority. Turkish residents also have the rights in Article 11 of Law No. 6698, including learning whether data is processed, recipients, purpose and use, requesting correction/deletion and notification to recipients, objecting to adverse results based solely on automated analysis, and seeking compensation for unlawful processing. We may ask only for information needed to verify the request. Never send a password, authentication ticket or one-time code. We respond within applicable statutory periods.

Please follow Steam eligibility rules and the game's regional age rating. This game is not directed at young children. If you believe a child has supplied personal information improperly, contact us. We will date changes to this notice and provide additional notice where required for a material change.

Sunucu maç/puan kayıtlarında şu anda yaşa bağlı otomatik silme süresi yoktur.
